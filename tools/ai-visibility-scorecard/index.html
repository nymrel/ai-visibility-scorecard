Questions, answered plainly

Before you ask

What is AI visibility?

Whether AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity can find, read, and cite your website when people ask them questions. It depends on your robots.txt rules, structured data, an llms.txt file, plain content, and off-site proof — the exact things this scorecard checks.

How does the scorecard work?

Tick every check your site already passes. It scores you 0 to 100, gives you a grade, and ranks the unchecked items by how much each would raise your score. Everything updates live as you tick — nothing is submitted anywhere.

Do I need a developer to fix these?

Some checks are copy edits you can do yourself, like writing an about page or plain headings. Others, like robots.txt and JSON-LD, may need a developer. The ranked list tells you what matters most first, so you spend any help where it counts.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you tick or type ever leaves your device — there is no server collecting it. Your answers are saved only in this browser so you can come back to them later.

What do I get in the $9 Pro Report?

A printable report built from the checks you ticked: every missing item written up in full, ordered by impact, with a copy-paste snippet for your stack (Next.js, WordPress, Shopify, or plain HTML). It comes with a calendar reminder dated 30 days out and a saved baseline file, so the re-check is a real before-and-after rather than a guess. Questions: contact@nymrel.com.
